01 / Botanical Origin

High altitude micro-climate

The prolonged maturation period at high elevations condenses essential sugars and chlorogenic acids within the bean structure. The physical bean tissue expands uniformly during the first crack, resulting in an exceptionally balanced cellular density that allows slow, gentle caramelization without surface scorching.

02 / The Thermal Curve

The delicate Maillard transition

Our cast iron drum roast balances radiant heat with turbulent hot air. We extend the drying phase to 4 minutes and 30 seconds before guiding the beans into the Maillard reaction, developing complex volatile terpenes without breaking the delicate essential oils that coat each bean’s cellular pore.
